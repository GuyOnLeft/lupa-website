Privacy Policy — Airtable Sync

Last updated 2 August 2026 · Airtable Sync is operated by Lupa Intel LLC.

Airtable Sync is a Shopify app that copies your store's product catalog into an Airtable base that you own and control. This policy describes exactly what the app stores, what it does not store, and how long it keeps it.

The short version

The app does not collect, store, or process personal data about your customers. It does not request access to orders, customers, checkouts, or any Shopify scope that would expose customer information. It reads products, inventory, and locations, and writes them to your Airtable base.

What the app stores

DataWhy
Your Shopify store domain and an access token issued by ShopifyTo read your catalog through Shopify's Admin API
Your Airtable API token, encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)To write into your base. It is never displayed again after you enter it
Your Airtable base ID, table IDs, and field mappingsTo know which Shopify field goes in which of your columns
A record of each sync: when it ran, how many records it covered, and which failed and whyTo power the Sync Health page so you can see whether syncing is working

What the app does not store

  • No customer names, emails, addresses, phone numbers, or order data.
  • No payment information. Billing is handled entirely by Shopify.
  • No copies of your product content. Product, variant, collection, and inventory data is read from Shopify and written to your Airtable base in the same operation. Sync records keep only a Shopify product identifier and a status message, not product content.

Who the data is shared with

Nobody. Data is exchanged only between Shopify, your Airtable base, and the app's own servers. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is not used to train machine-learning models. Infrastructure is provided by Render (United States), which stores the data described above on the app's behalf.

How long data is kept

  • Sync history and per-record results: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Completed background jobs: 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Your connection settings and field mappings: for as long as the app is installed.
  • When you uninstall, everything belonging to your store is deleted, via Shopify's shop/redact request.

Security

  • Your Airtable token is encrypted before it is stored and is never shown again.
  • All connections use TLS, including between the app and its database, which refuses unencrypted connections.
  • Requests from Shopify are verified cryptographically (HMAC); unverified requests are rejected.
  • Error messages shown in the app are written by the app, so infrastructure details are never exposed.

Your rights

You can disconnect Airtable at any time from within the app, or uninstall the app to delete all of your data. Shopify's privacy requests — data access, customer redaction, and shop redaction — are all supported. Because the app holds no customer personal data, a customer data request returns nothing to disclose.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated and the change described in the app's listing.

Contact

Lupa Intel LLC — questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the support address listed on the app's Shopify App Store page.
